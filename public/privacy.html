1. About NexTap

NexTap (“NexTap,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides NFC-enabled digital business cards and digital profile services. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and handle personal information when you use our website, purchase or use a NexTap card, create a digital profile, or interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your use of NexTap, we may collect account information such as your name, email address, login credentials, and account details; profile information such as your name, business name, job title, photo, phone number, email, website, social links, portfolio links, and business information; card and technical information such as card identifiers, QR/profile links, card status, and basic technical information; and order information such as name, contact, delivery, order, and payment-related information.

3. Public Profile Information

Information you intentionally publish on your NexTap profile may be accessible to anyone who receives your profile link, scans your QR code, or taps your NFC card. Only publish information you are comfortable making public. Do not publish passwords, financial credentials, government identification numbers, or confidential information.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information to create and manage accounts and profiles, provide NFC and QR functionality, process orders, provide support, improve and secure NexTap, prevent fraud or abuse, and comply with applicable laws.

6. Sharing

NexTap may use cookies, local storage, analytics, or similar technologies to operate, secure, analyze, and improve the website. Where required by law, appropriate notice or consent mechanisms may be provided.

8. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, business records, dispute resolution, security, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. When no longer necessary, information may be deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of subject to legal requirements.

9. Security

10. Your Rights
