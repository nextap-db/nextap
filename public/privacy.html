NexTap provides physical NFC cards and digital profiles. This notice covers our website, orders, client accounts, profiles, and support.

01Information we handle

Order and delivery details
Your name, email, phone, selected contact channels, delivery address and notes, printed card name and title, plan, quantity, design choice, artwork requests, and order amounts. These support order review and fulfilment.
Profile content
The information you or NexTap add to your profile, such as your name, role, company, photo, contact details, social links, about text, content blocks, gallery images, and business information.
Account and security information
Your sign-in identifier, client password hash, account status, and session information. Sign-in abuse checks use short-lived counters with hashed account and network identifiers.
Basic profile activity
A profile view total and its latest view time. These figures do not identify individual viewers or show a list of unique visitors.

Profile photos and submitted reference images may be stored with the relevant record. Send only material needed for your profile or order.

02Why we use it

Information is used to review and fulfil orders, arrange client access, publish and update profiles, answer support requests, protect accounts, prevent abuse, and maintain records where needed.

The applicable legal basis depends on the purpose: providing a requested service or performing a contract, your consent where relevant, a legal obligation, or a permitted legitimate interest such as account security. The Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 explains lawful processing requirements.

Submitting an order records your request for manual review. It does not take a payment or automatically create or activate a Client Dashboard account.

03Your profile is public

Share only what you want people to see.

Anyone with an active profile link can open it, including someone who taps your NFC card or scans its QR code. A card or QR code is a way to share a profile, not an account password.

Public content may be copied, photographed, redistributed, or indexed by another service. Keep passwords, one-time codes, government identification numbers, financial credentials, and confidential information off your profile.

Use the Client Dashboard to edit available profile fields. For deactivation or deletion, follow the data request process.

04Providers and external services

NexTap uses Cloudflare Workers to serve the website and Cloudflare D1 to store service records. Private deployment and maintenance checks also temporarily process service data in GitHub Actions. Details necessary for delivery and support may be shared with the people or providers handling those tasks, or disclosed when lawfully required.

If configured, order notifications use Resend email and Meta WhatsApp. They can include customer contact information, delivery address and notes, printed name and title, selected items, design codes, and amounts. These notifications do not include account passwords or submitted reference images.

  • The QR preview service at api.qrserver.com receives the public profile URL to generate its code.
  • Some sign-in, order, and profile pages request fonts from Google Fonts.
  • External profile images contact their hosts when loaded. External links contact their services when followed. Those services have their own privacy notices.
  • Delivery address lookup uses a server proxy to PSGC Cloud with geographic codes, rather than your name, email, or street address.

For questions about a particular recipient or an order notification, contact NexTap.

05Cookies and browser storage

Sign-in cookies
Client and administrator sign-ins use signed cookies with a seven-day expiry. They are marked Secure and HttpOnly. Signing out clears the session cookie in that browser.
Saved cart
Local storage remembers plan choices, quantities, the own-artwork option, and design codes. It does not store your delivery details, contact details, or password.
Display preferences
Local storage can remember light or dark mode and administrator menu preferences. These browser values have no automatic expiry.

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. This can sign you out or remove saved cart and display preferences. Essential cookies are needed for account access.

06Security and retention

Client passwords are stored as salted hashes, and authenticated requests are checked before account changes. See Account Security for practical steps and the current protections. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Retention depends on the information and its purpose. Profile and account information support the service; order and support records may remain necessary for fulfilment, disputes, security, or legal obligations. The current service does not automatically purge client or order records.

Requests are reviewed manually, including relevant retained copies and backups where applicable. Deleting a profile record does not by itself delete a separate order or a notification held by another provider. NexTap can explain the scope, applicable retention needs, and next steps when reviewing your request.

07Your privacy rights

Under applicable Philippine privacy law, you may exercise rights to be informed, access your personal data, correct inaccuracies, object to processing where applicable, seek erasure or blocking when warranted, request data portability where applicable, and seek remedies for a privacy violation.

You may also withdraw consent for processing that relies on consent. This can affect the related service; information still needed for another lawful purpose may be retained.

The National Privacy Commission explains data subject rights. Its erasure and blocking guidance describes the conditions and lawful limits on those requests. You may raise a concern with NexTap or follow the Commission’s complaint process.

08Questions, corrections, and updates

Email nextapph@gmail.com with the type of request and the account email, profile link, or order reference needed to locate your information. Ownership checks may be necessary before access or changes.

Do not send your password, one-time codes, or full payment credentials. If your concern involves a child’s information or information published without authorization, explain that in your message.
