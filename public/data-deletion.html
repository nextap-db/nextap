Data Deletion & Update Process

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

1. Update Your Information

Where profile editing is available, update your information through your NexTap account. You may update profile details such as your name, photo, business name, job title, phone, email, website, social links, portfolio links, and other profile information.

2. Request an Update

If you cannot make a change through your account, email nextapph@gmail.com with your name, account email, the information to correct, and the requested change.

3. Deactivate Your Profile

You may request that your public NexTap profile be deactivated. Contact nextapph@gmail.com with the subject “NexTap Profile Deactivation Request.”

4. Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your NexTap account and associated personal information, email nextapph@gmail.com with your full name, account email address, and a description of the account or information you want deleted.

5. Verification

For security, we may request reasonable information to verify your identity or account ownership before processing an update, deactivation, or deletion request.

6. After Deletion

Where deletion is appropriate and legally permitted, we may disable your account, disable your digital profile, remove or anonymize personal information, and disable associated profile access mechanisms.

7. Information We May Retain

Certain information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations, maintain required records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, establish or defend legal claims, maintain security, or fulfill another lawful purpose. Where retention is necessary, use will be limited to the applicable purpose.

8. NFC Card and QR Code

Deleting or disabling your account/profile may affect the NFC card and QR code associated with it. The previous profile may no longer be displayed.

9. Public Copies

Deletion from NexTap cannot remove copies that other people previously viewed, downloaded, saved, screenshotted, or redistributed.

10. Data Portability

Where applicable, you may request a copy of personal information processed about you in a structured and commonly used electronic format by contacting nextapph@gmail.com.

11. Privacy Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact NexTap first so we can investigate. You may also have rights to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.
