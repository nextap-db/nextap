A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your
first tap.

Here’s how your card and profile come together.

What comes with my NexTap card?

A physical NFC card with your selected design and a digital profile. The Basic, Premium, and Elite plan descriptions show the profile content offered with each plan.

Can I choose any design for any plan?

Yes. All 36 collection designs are available for Basic, Premium, and Elite. Each design has a matching front and back.

Does Customized Design include the own-artwork add-on?

C-series Customized Design cards are premade collection designs included in the card price. If you would like to use your own artwork or request a custom design, select the optional ₱49 add-on per card at checkout.

Are Animated Design cards animated?

Animated Design is the name of our illustrated card collection. The previews show the printed artwork on the front and back of the card.

Do I need an app or an NFC phone?

A compatible NFC phone can open your profile with a tap. Your profile can also be opened through its QR code or direct link in a browser.

Can I update my profile later?

Yes. Use the Client Dashboard to update your profile information. The card continues to connect people to that profile.

What counts as a content block?

Each published section with content counts once, including Featured, Services, Portfolio, or Gallery. Basic allows 3 blocks, Premium 6, and Elite unlimited. Your name, photo, contact details, and social links do not use block slots. Your admin chooses which sections are available. Hidden drafts stay saved without using slots. Existing profiles above their limit keep their published content; adding a new section requires getting within the limit or changing plans.

Does ordering create my login or complete payment?

Submitting checkout records your order. NexTap reviews it and contacts you about confirmation, payment, and profile setup. Your client account is activated separately; checkout does not sign you in or process a card payment.

Who can see my profile?

Anyone with your published profile link, card tap, or QR code can view the information you make public. Keep passwords, verification codes, and private documents out of your profile. See our Privacy Notice for more about your data.

How do I get login help or request a data change?

For account activation or a password reset, contact NexTap. Never send your password or verification codes. Our data update and deletion guide explains how to request a correction, profile deactivation, data copy, or deletion.

How much is shipping, and what happens after I order?

Shipping is ₱70 per order within Luzon or ₱99 for Visayas and Mindanao. NexTap reviews your order and contacts you to confirm the details and next steps.
