FAQ

Simple by design.

Do I need an app?

No. NexTap is designed so a compatible phone can open your profile through NFC or the profile link. QR code access is also available.

Can I update my profile?

Yes. Your digital information can be updated without replacing the physical card.

Can people without NFC use it?

Yes. Your NexTap profile can also be shared through its link and QR code.

What can my profile contain?

Contact details, social links, websites, portfolio, services, business information, location, payment options, booking and other supported profile features.
