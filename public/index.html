OUR STORY

Built for Filipino professionals and entrepreneurs.

NexTap started with a simple frustration: running out of paper business cards at the exact moment they mattered most. We built NexTap for the freelancers, agents, business owners, and sales teams across the Philippines who wanted a sharper way to make a first impression — one tap, and everything about you and your business is right there. No app to install, no reprinting when your number or title changes, just a card that keeps working for you long after the meeting ends.

FAQ

Got questions? We've got answers.

Do I need to download an app to use NexTap?

No app is needed. Whoever you tap your card against just needs a phone with NFC turned on — most smartphones from the last several years already support this by default.

Is my phone compatible with NexTap?

Most modern iPhones and Android phones support NFC out of the box. If NFC isn't automatically detected, it can usually be turned on in your phone's settings.

Can I update my information after I order?

Yes. Your NexTap card links to a digital profile you can update anytime — your contact details, socials, and links can change without ever needing a new card.

Is my data safe?

We take reasonable safeguards to protect the information on your profile, and you're always in control of what's shared and visible.

Can I customize my card's design?

Yes — choose from the NexTap Classic, Gold, Black, or Premium finishes, and message us on WhatsApp if you'd like to ask about custom designs.

How do I order a NexTap card?

Message us directly on WhatsApp using the button below and we'll walk you through choosing your card and setting up your profile.
