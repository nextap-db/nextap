SECURITY

Account Security

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

1. Protect Your Login

Use a strong, unique password, never share it, avoid password reuse, log out on shared computers, and keep your email account secure. NexTap personnel should not request your complete password.

2. Protect Your Email

Your email may be used for account access or recovery. Use a strong email password and available security features such as two-factor authentication.

3. Lost or Stolen NFC Card

If your NexTap card is lost or stolen, contact nextapph@gmail.com as soon as possible. Where available, we may disable or change the profile associated with the card.

4. NFC Cards Are Not Passwords

A NexTap card is designed to share a digital profile. It should not be treated as a password, security token, or protection for confidential information. Do not place passwords, financial credentials, authentication codes, government ID numbers, or confidential information on a public profile.

5. QR Code Security

Your QR code can direct people to your public profile and can be photographed or shared. Treat it as a public link and publish only information you are comfortable sharing.

6. Suspicious Activity

Contact us if you notice unexpected account changes, suspicious login activity, an unexpected password reset, unauthorized profile changes, or other activity you believe is unauthorized.

7. NexTap Security Measures

NexTap uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and personal information. No online system can guarantee complete security.

8. Security Incidents

If NexTap becomes aware of a security incident involving personal information, we will assess it and take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law and our security procedures. Where legally required, affected users and/or authorities may be notified.

9. Account Recovery

If you lose access to your account, contact nextapph@gmail.com. We may request information to verify that you are the legitimate account owner before restoring access.
