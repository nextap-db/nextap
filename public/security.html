A NexTap card shares your public profile. Account access is protected separately by your client credentials and sign-in session.

01Protect your login

  • Use a strong password that you do not use for another service.
  • Keep your password private and sign out when using a shared device.
  • Keep the email account associated with your client access secure. Use that email provider’s two-factor authentication if available.
  • Check the website address before entering credentials. Open the Client Login from NexTap’s site.
Support does not need your password.

Do not send NexTap your password, one-time authentication codes, or full payment credentials.

02Current account protections

Stored client passwords
Client passwords are stored as salted hashes rather than plain text.
Protected sign-in sessions
Signed client session cookies are marked Secure and HttpOnly, use SameSite=Lax, and expire after seven days. Signing out clears the cookie in that browser.
Server checks
Account changes require an authenticated session. Sign-in and password-change attempts have abuse checks.
Password changes
A successful client password change or administrator reset invalidates prior client sessions. A password change while signed in requires the current password.

These measures reduce risk; no online system guarantees complete security. Client access currently uses an email or phone identifier and password, without a NexTap two-factor sign-in flow.

03Password reset and access help

If you cannot sign in, need a password reset, or are waiting for activation, email nextapph@gmail.com. Recovery and activation are handled manually by NexTap.

Include your account email or phone identifier and your profile link if available. NexTap may ask for reasonable additional information to verify ownership before changing account access.

If you can sign in and know your current password, use the password-change option in the Client Dashboard.

04A lost card, QR code, or public link

Your card and QR code open a public profile; possession of them does not grant access to your Client Dashboard. A QR code can be photographed and a profile link can be forwarded.

For a lost or stolen card, email nextapph@gmail.com with your profile link and the request. NexTap can review manual deactivation of the linked profile.

While the account is deactivated, its public profile is unavailable through the current link, card, and QR code. Client sign-in and existing sessions also cannot access the account while it is inactive. It does not recover the physical card or erase copies already saved elsewhere. See deactivation and deletion differences.

05Publish with care

Keep passwords, financial credentials, government identification numbers, one-time codes, and confidential documents out of your public profile. Review contact fields, photos, links, and uploaded content before publishing.

External links, images, QR preview services, and linked payment or booking instructions involve other providers. Check those destinations and their terms before sharing information with them. Read more in the Privacy Policy.

06Report suspicious activity

If you notice unexpected profile changes, unfamiliar account activity, suspicious messages, or a security problem, email nextapph@gmail.com.

Describe what happened, when you noticed it, and which profile or account is affected. Include relevant screenshots with sensitive details removed. If you still have access and suspect your password is exposed, change it.

NexTap assesses reported incidents and takes appropriate steps. Affected people or authorities are notified where required by applicable law.
