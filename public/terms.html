01Cards and digital profiles

A NexTap card directs people to a digital profile using NFC or a QR code where available. An internet connection, compatible device, browser, and supported NFC settings may be needed.

All 36 collection designs are available with Basic, Premium, and Elite. The website’s plan descriptions explain the profile content offered. Animated Design is the name of a collection of printed artwork.

Profiles can contain external links, including booking information or payment instructions. These are links or published content; NexTap does not operate a booking calendar or payment gateway through the card.

02Client access and account care

Client accounts are created and activated by NexTap after review. An order submission does not create an account or automatically activate access.

Provide accurate information and keep your credentials private. Use a unique password, sign out on shared devices, and contact NexTap if you lose access or believe someone has changed your account.

Signed-in clients can change their password using the current password. Password resets and activation help are handled manually through support. See Account Security.

A profile link can be shared.

Anyone with an active profile link, NFC card, or QR code can view its public content. Publish only information you are comfortable sharing. See the public profile explanation.

You retain ownership of content you own. NexTap needs to use the materials you submit to provide the requested card and profile service.
